Design principle

We do not confuse attendance history with capability.

Route A

Completed DFQI Part 1

Continue to Part 2 when ready. If the diagnostic shows weaker areas, use a targeted refresher instead of being unnecessarily excluded.

Route B

Did not attend Part 1, but already ready

A strong readiness result opens a direct-entry/equivalency review rather than forcing experienced professionals to repeat foundational learning.

Route C

Foundation still developing

Enter the Foundation Bridge or the next Part 1 cohort and retain your original Part 2 intent so the system can bring you back when ready.

The Part 2 promise

Part 1 gave you the foundation. Part 2 is about doing the work.

Knowing the name of a QI tool is different from knowing which one fits the problem in front of you. Understanding a run chart is different from interpreting variation correctly and deciding what to do next.

Part 2 is designed to close that application gap through live learning, guided practice and coaching.

What the pathway protects

Free value without giving away Part 2

The free community can provide practical frontline tools, demonstrations, Q&A and proof-of-practice tasks. The advanced Part 2 curriculum and coaching remain behind the programme boundary.

Campaign air-traffic-control: DFQI promotion should not compete publicly with another SQHN offer aimed at the same audience. The system can still capture and nurture DFQI interest quietly through direct outreach, WhatsApp and targeted channels.

What happens next?

  1. Your interest is captured in the DFQI CRM.
  2. The team confirms the appropriate pathway or equivalency review.
  3. Any administrative registration process happens outside this system.
  4. Once confirmed, the team updates your programme status and sends onboarding instructions.
No payment processing: this product intentionally does not collect card or banking details, run payment gateways or issue financial receipts.

Privacy & data use

Information submitted through this system is used to assess readiness, recommend a DFQI pathway, manage programme interest, engagement and follow-up, and understand which outreach channels are working. Readiness results are programme-routing indicators, not professional credentials or clinical assessments. Card, banking and payment details are not collected or processed here. Access to identifiable programme records is restricted to authorised programme administrators.
